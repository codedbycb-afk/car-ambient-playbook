The verdict

Sell the acrylic "Symphony" ambient kit (6-in-1 / 10-in-1 / 18-in-1), app + music sync, shipped from Cleveland in 2–3 days.

It is the one product in the car-lighting category that is proven on every channel at once (Amazon, TikTok Shop, Meta DTC stores), still has a 4–6x markup between factory cost and DTC price, and has an obvious weak spot in every competitor (3.8–4.1 star ratings, 12–24% one-star reviews, almost all about install, adhesive and the app). You win by fixing the install experience, not by inventing a product.

1. What changed since you last did this

Your old loop (Alibaba/AliExpress → Shopify → supplier ships each order from China) is structurally broken in 2026. Four things changed:

  1. The $800 duty-free exemption is gone. CBP's June 24, 2026 interim final rules suspended de minimis for every country and every shipping mode, including postal mail. Every parcel from China now owes duty (Section 301 25% + the Section 122 surcharge, stacked on the base rate). A single $15 kit mailed to a customer can arrive with a duty bill or get stuck at a postal informal entry. Customers who get a surprise bill charge back.
  2. Delivery expectations moved to 5–7 days. 2–3 week China transit is now the #1 driver of refunds and chargebacks, and Shopify Payments holds funds on new stores for 7–14 days anyway. Speed is the whole game.
  3. TikTok Shop became a real channel. Sellers list once, creators promote for commission (affiliate), and TikTok handles checkout. But it demands dispatch within 2 business days with real tracking, so it only works if the product is already in the US.
  4. Meta moved to Advantage+ / Andromeda. Audience targeting matters less; creative volume and a clean pixel + Conversions API feed matter more. The testing unit is the creative, not the audience.
The 2026 model that fits you: buy a small batch (20–30 kits, ~$500–800) shipped DDP to Cleveland by air, hold it at home, ship each order via Shopify Shipping (USPS Ground Advantage, ~$7–9). You said you'd fulfill anyway. This is the real advantage: 2–3 day delivery, branded unboxing, zero customs surprises, and TikTok Shop eligibility. CJdropshipping/Zendrop US warehouses are the fallback if you'd rather not touch boxes, but their US stock of this exact SKU is spotty and the margin is ~$10/unit worse.

What makes you different (pick all four, they're cheap)

  • Install videos per car type (sedan, SUV, truck, Tesla). Film them yourselves on your own cars and friends' cars. This is the content engine and the support desk in one.
  • "Stays stuck" kit: include a plastic trim tool, alcohol wipes and 3M VHB pads. Costs ~$2, kills the #1 one-star reason.
  • Ships from Ohio, 2–3 days. Say it on every ad and page. It is the single biggest difference from Amazon's Chinese sellers and from any China-direct dropshipper.
  • 30-day fit guarantee. Doesn't fit your trim gap, send it back. Real returns on this product run low when the install video is good.
Brand name: stay away from "Glow" anything (GlowRide, CruiseGlow, RideGlow already exist). Pick something you can trademark and say out loud in a TikTok. Check the .com and the TikTok handle in the same sitting.

4. The 8-week plan

Assumes you both start this week. CB owns tech, store and ad account; sister owns product, filming and fulfillment. Everything is checkboxes so you can work from this page.

00this week

Decide and order

  • Order 1 acrylic kit on Amazon today (MAODANER 6-in-1 or Jushope 10-in-1) so filming starts in 2 days, not 2 weeks. sister
  • Message 3 Alibaba suppliers from the table above. Ask: DDP price to Cleveland 44xxx for 2 samples now and 30 units later, lead time, whether they can add your logo on the controller and box at 30 units, and whether their app is their own or a white-label one (ask the app's name). Order 2 samples each from the best 2. sister
  • Pick the brand name, buy the .com, grab TikTok + IG handles. both
  • Open Shopify (3-day trial, then $1/mo for 3 months, then Basic $39). Use Shopify Payments; make sure the legal name matches the bank account or payouts get held. CB
  • Sole prop with EIN is enough to start; form the LLC once you have 30 days of sales. Whoever is 18+ with a US bank account owns the Shopify, Meta and TikTok Shop accounts (TikTok Shop requires 18+). CB
01wk 1–2

Film while you wait

  • Install the Amazon kit on one car. Film everything on a phone: unboxing, trim-tool install, the "lights on" reveal at dusk, the app, music sync, a driving POV at night. Shoot vertical, 4K, clean audio. sister
  • Get 15–20 raw clips. You need 6–8 finished ads in week 3, each 15–30 seconds, each with a different first 3 seconds. sister
  • Build the store: one hero product page with the 3 kit variants, add-ons, a 60-second install video, before/after gallery, FAQ (fits my car? glare? adhesive? shipping?), reviews section, guarantee. Pages: shipping, returns, contact. No menu bloat. CB
  • Install the Meta Sales Channel app and the TikTok app in Shopify. Create Meta Business Manager, Page, pixel, dataset; turn on Conversions API via the Shopify app; verify the domain. Create TikTok Shop seller account and TikTok Ads account. CB
  • Set up Shopify Shipping with USPS Ground Advantage, 2-day handling. Buy 30 mailer boxes (6x4x3 or whatever the kit needs) and a thermal label printer (~$120). sister
02wk 2–3

Samples arrive, decide the supplier, place the batch

  • Test all samples on 2 different cars: adhesive after a hot day, app pairing, brightness consistency, strip length vs real trim gaps. Pick the one with the best app and brightest, most even acrylic. sister
  • Order 30 units DDP by air (~$600–900 all-in). Ask for your logo on the box insert at minimum. Lead time 7–15 days. both
  • Re-shoot the hero content with the real kit and box. Cut the 6–8 ad variants. Write primary text and headlines (CB's ads agents do this). both
  • List the product on TikTok Shop too, open an affiliate plan at 15–20% commission, and target 20 small car creators through the Affiliate Center. Free distribution while ads warm up. CB
03wk 3–4

Launch ads (testing phase)

  • Day 1 of inventory in hand = day 1 of ads, not before. CB
  • One Advantage+ Sales campaign, broad US 18–45, $40–50/day, 6–8 creatives in. One separate Test ad set at $20/day for new hooks each week. Judge the winning ad's spend, not the campaign average. CB
  • TikTok Ads: $20/day Spark Ads on the best organic post once one gets traction. CB
  • Post 3–5 organic TikToks and Reels a week from the same footage. Organic retargets ad viewers for free and drops CPMs. sister
  • Ship every order same or next day. Send a tracking SMS/email with the install video link. sister
04wk 4–6

Read, kill, double down

  • Give each creative 7 days and ~$70 before killing. Kill rule: no add-to-carts after $40, or CPA over $75 after 7 days. Keep rule: CPA under $45 on 3+ purchases. CB
  • Ship 2–3 new hook variations of the winner each week (same body, new first 3 seconds). sister
  • Reorder 50–100 units at the lower ladder price the day you have 10 units left. sister
  • Ask every buyer for a video review in exchange for the $19 footwell add-on. Buyer footage is next month's ads. sister
06wk 6–8

Scale what works

  • Raise budget 20% every 3 days on the winning campaign while CPA holds. Never double overnight. CB
  • Add a Winners campaign (proven creatives only) at 60% of spend; 30% to variations; 10% to fresh concepts. CB
  • Add the starlight headliner as a post-purchase upsell email and a retargeting-only product. Don't run it cold. CB
  • Open vehicle-specific pages (Tesla Model 3/Y, Camry, Civic, F-150) with their own install video. Search traffic and lower CPA. both

5. Timeline to traction, honestly

Day 3–5of ads: first real signal (CTR above 1.5%, add-to-carts). Not sales yet.
Week 1–2of ads: first orders. Expect to lose money. This is tuition.
Week 4–6of ads: a repeatable creative with CPA under $45. This is "traction".
~Week 8–10from today: consistently profitable days if the product and creative are right.

Budget for the first 60 days: $1,500–2,500 in ads, $700–900 inventory, ~$250 store, apps, boxes, printer. Roughly $2,500–3,600 to find out properly. If you can't fund the ad testing through week 6 without panicking, wait until you can; the most common way this fails is killing ads on day 3.

Your hooks (Wehner's conditions + beliefs framing, from CB's ads brain)

Lead with the situation the buyer is already in, not the label "car owners". In-market people are already googling "ambient lighting kit"; needs-convincing people have a dark cabin and don't know this exists for under $500.

  • "If your car's interior is pitch black at night, this took me 20 minutes." (needs-convincing, situational)
  • "I almost paid a shop $500 for ambient lighting. Then I did this in my driveway." (price-anchor contrast)
  • "Why every Amazon ambient kit falls off in summer, and the $2 fix." (one-star reviews become your ad)
  • "Ships from Ohio. At your door in 2 days. No waiting 3 weeks for China." (the structural advantage, said plainly)
  • POV night drive with music sync, no voiceover, caption only. (pure visual; this is the TikTok Shop winner format)

Shot list (one afternoon, one car, phone only)

  • Dark cabin "before" at dusk → the reveal (same angle, 3 seconds each).
  • Hands tucking the acrylic strip into the dash gap with the trim tool, close up.
  • Phone screen: color picker, brightness, music mode responding to a beat.
  • Passenger-seat POV driving at night, city lights outside, cabin glowing.
  • Unboxing: box, insert, trim tool, VHB pads, QR to install video.
  • 30 seconds of sister talking to camera: "what I'd tell you before you buy one of these."

7. Handing CB the ad account

Do it as a partner grant, not a shared password. It survives 2FA, keeps billing on your card, and CB's Meta Ads tools plug straight in.

  1. Create your own Meta Business Portfolio (business.facebook.com) under the owner's personal profile with 2FA on. Create the Page, the ad account and the pixel/dataset inside it. Add a payment method and set a spending limit.
  2. Business Settings → Users → Partners → Add → "Give a partner access to your assets". Enter Twenty47 Marketing's Business ID. Assign: Ad account (Manage campaigns / Advertiser), Page (Advertise + Create content), Pixel (Manage), Catalog (Manage), Instagram account (Content + Ads). Do not grant Admin on the business.
  3. In Shopify, the Meta Sales Channel app connects to that same Business Portfolio and turns on Conversions API. Confirm Events Manager shows Purchase events with "Good" match quality within 48 hours.
  4. Verify the domain in Brand Safety → Domains (add the TXT record at the registrar).
  5. TikTok: Business Center → Members → invite CB's email as Admin on the Ads account; in TikTok Shop, add CB as a sub-account with Marketing permission.
  6. Give CB a Shopify staff account (Orders, Products, Analytics, Apps; not Billing).
What CB then runs: campaign structure (Advantage+ Sales + a Test ad set), budget pacing, the 7-day kill/keep rules above, weekly creative requests back to you with exactly which hook to reshoot, and a Sunday numbers note: spend, orders, CPA, ROAS, what's next. You create the ads and ship the boxes. Nothing else.

8. Things that quietly kill new stores

  • Shipping late. TikTok Shop throttles you above a 2-day dispatch. Shopify Payments holds funds when disputes pass ~1%. Ship same day. Always real tracking.
  • Overpromising "fits every car". Say "fits most cars with a dash or door trim gap of 1–3mm" and show the gap test in the video. Honest fit claims cut returns in half.
  • Brightness glare. Reddit's one real objection. Film the windshield at night to show there's none at medium brightness.
  • Running out of stock during a winning week. Reorder at 10 units left, every time.
  • Judging ads at day 3. Seven days, $70, then decide.
  • Sales tax. Turn on Shopify Tax; you have Ohio nexus from day one. It handles the rest until you cross other states' thresholds.
  • Supplier's white-label app disappearing. Ask which app the controller uses; prefer suppliers on a widely used one (Tuya/Smart Life based), not a one-off.
