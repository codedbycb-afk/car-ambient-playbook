  • Turn on Shopify Payments. The legal name on Shopify must match the bank account name or payouts get held.

3Film the first kitWeek 1 · 1 afternoon, 1 car, your phonesister

Install the Amazon kit and film everything vertical in 4K. You need 15–20 clips. Each ad later is 15–30 seconds.

  • Dark cabin at dusk → lights-on reveal, same angle.
  • Hands tucking the strip into the dash gap with the trim tool, close up.
  • Phone screen: picking a colour, brightness, music mode reacting to a song.
  • Passenger-seat view driving at night.
  • Unboxing: box, strip, controller, trim tool, VHB pads.
  • 30 seconds of you talking: "what I'd tell you before you buy one of these."
  • Film the windshield at night to show there's no glare at medium brightness (it's the #1 Reddit objection).

How to install it: YouTube: acrylic ambient light install tutorials (watch 2–3 before you start; brands differ slightly).

6Pick the supplier, order 30 unitsWeek 2–3 · when samples landboth
  • Test every sample on 2 cars: does the adhesive hold after a hot day, does the app pair first try, is the strip evenly bright, does it fit a real trim gap.
  • Ask each factory which app the controller uses. Prefer Tuya / Smart Life based apps (they won't vanish). Avoid a one-off app.
  • Order 30 of the winner, DDP by air (duties paid, ~$600–900 all-in, 7–15 days). Ask for your logo on the box insert. Pay through Alibaba Trade Assurance only.
  • Buy shipping supplies: 6x4x3 mailer boxes (check your kit's box size first) and a 4x6 thermal label printer (~$100–150).
  • Set up shipping in Shopify: Shopify Shipping with USPS → use Ground Advantage (2–5 days, ~$7–9). Set handling time to 1 day.

8Wait 7 days before judging anythingWeek 3–6 · the part everyone gets wrongCB
  • Each ad gets 7 days and ~$70 before it's killed. Not 3 days.
  • Kill: no add-to-carts after $40 spent, or over $75 per sale after 7 days. Keep: under $45 per sale on 3+ sales.
  • Every week, reshoot 2–3 new openings for the winning ad. Same body, new first 3 seconds. SISTER
  • Reorder 50–100 units the day you hit 10 left (price drops to ~$15/unit at 100). SISTER
  • Offer every buyer the $19 footwell pack free for a 30-second video review. Their footage is next month's ads.

What to expect: first real signal (clicks, add-to-carts) by day 3–5. First orders in week 1–2 of ads, probably at a loss. A repeatable ad under $45 per sale by week 4–6 of ads. Consistently profitable days around week 8–10 from today. Budget to get there: $1,500–2,500 in ads + $700–900 inventory + ~$250 setup. If you can't fund 6 weeks of ads without stopping early, wait until you can.

9Scale what worksWeek 6+ CB
  • Raise the winning campaign's budget 20% every 3 days while cost per sale holds. Never double overnight.
  • Split spend 60% proven ads / 30% variations / 10% brand-new ideas.
  • Add the starlight headliner kit as an email upsell to past buyers only (don't run it to cold traffic; 3-day install = returns).
  • Make car-specific pages (Tesla Model 3/Y, Camry, Civic, F-150) each with its own install video.

Don't do these

  • Don't ship from China per order. The $800 duty-free rule ended for everything in June 2026 (Federal Register notice). Customers get duty bills, wait 3 weeks, and charge back.
  • Don't say "fits every car". Say "fits most cars with a 1–3 mm dash or door trim gap" and show the gap test.
  • Don't kill an ad on day 3.
  • Don't run out of stock in a winning week.
  • Don't sell the cheap USB 4-strip kits. Govee owns that (80,000 reviews at $16.99).
